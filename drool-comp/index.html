Our Story

Made for the Hungry & the Bold

Tucked in the heart of Ponda near the old market, The Drool Company is Goa's go-to destination for next-level burgers. We're not your average fast food — we're a full-on flavour experience.

From the iconic Chipotle Crunch Burger to our Butter Chicken Fries and signature shakes, every item on our menu is crafted to make you come back. Always Bhookha? We've got you covered.
